I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Applied Mathematics at **The Hong Kong Polytechnic University**. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics, specialising in Computational Mathematics, from **Peking University** in 2023, and my B.S. in Mathematics from **Jilin University** in 2018.

My research focuses on numerical methods for partial differential equations, particularly finite element methods for geometric problems and geometric flows. My recent work includes Ricci flow, isometric embedding of Riemannian manifolds, mean curvature flow, and surface diffusion. I am also interested in fully nonlinear equations, auxiliary space preconditioners, non-standard finite element methods.

Here is my [CV]({{ '/assets/pdf/CV.pdf' | relative_url }}).

## Research interests

- Intrinsic geometric problems and finite element tensor calculus.
- PDEs on surfaces, free boundary problems, and geometric flows.
- Elliptic equations in non-divergence form and fully nonlinear PDEs, including Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman and Monge–Ampère equations.
- Auxiliary space preconditioners and fast solvers for finite element discretizations.
- Structure-preserving numerical methods
- Non-standard finite element methods.
